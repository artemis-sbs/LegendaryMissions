# Crew Rosters {: #lm-crew-rosters}

The stock crews. A mission that loads this addon can pick one from the server console's \*\*Crew\*\* dropdown, or pin one with \`Defaults: CREW_SELECT\` on its \`@map\`, and every console nobody named arrives with somebody at it.

None of these bind to a hull or a ship name, deliberately - they are offered, never imposed. A mod that wants its own crew on its own hulls declares \`Hull:\` and needs none of this.

\`Gender:\` is here because a face is ROLLED for anyone with no \`Face:\` of their own, and a terran face has a gender axis - so without it the roll is a coin toss against the name above it. It says which face, and nothing else.

## TSN Watch {: #lm-crew-rosters-tsn-watch}

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Race | terran |

A Terran Stellar Navy watch bill - the default answer for a TSN hull.

Deep enough to crew a whole ship, not just a bridge. The first seven name their station; the rest name none, which makes them FLOATING officers - \`\_seat_pick\` offers a member with no \`Console:\` to any seat nobody was written for. A game with a dozen consoles open, a second science station and a hangar full of pilots therefore stays inside this cast instead of falling through to automatic names half way down the ship.
